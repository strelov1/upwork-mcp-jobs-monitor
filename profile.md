every run — no need to duplicate them here. This file only holds preferences Upwork's API has
no way of knowing.

## What to avoid
- Skip anything where `activityStat.totalHired >= 1` on the job posting — already filled or filling.
- Skip hourly jobs under $25/hr unless the client is clearly strong (verified, real spend/reviews)
  and the scope is short and low-effort.
- Skip jobs that require deep, specific domain expertise I don't actually have — e.g. Informatica
  IDMC / enterprise metadata lineage / graph-database architecture, blockchain/Web3, iGaming
  compliance, niche no-code builders (Bubble.io, Adalo). Don't stretch my real experience to fit.
- Skip clients with $0 total spent AND zero reviews AND a vague/templated job post — high flake risk.
- Skip anything explicitly full-time exclusive (30-40+ hrs/week, "not for you if you have other
  clients") — I already carry ~30-40 hrs/week of committed capacity on an active contract, so a
  second full-time exclusive role is a real conflict, not just a scheduling inconvenience. Flag
  these instead of drafting a proposal that promises availability I don't have.

## Tone
- Direct and concise. No filler ("I'd love to help", "Dear Hiring Manager", "professional developer
  with N years", "I'm excited to..."). No emojis unless the client's own post uses them.
- Open with the client's problem or the first thing they ask for, not with me. Answer their
  questions in the order they asked them before anything else.
- Ground every claim in real, named work: diffray.ai (30-agent PR review pipeline, <2min/review),
  PrivatClaw (Telegram bot SaaS, 3s provisioning), Telagon (Go + ClickHouse analytics, 700K+
  channels), and the enterprise LLM pipeline inside a 150+ microservice platform at 99.999% SLA.
  Never invent a project, a number, or a tool I haven't actually used.
